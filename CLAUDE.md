# Insieme (pokemon-together)

A bilingual Italian/English, child-friendly tutorial PWA for the physical Pokémon Trading Card Game. Personal project.

@README.md

It's a git repo with its own remote, `github.com/jordancox/pokemon-together`. `dist/` and `hosting.json` are gitignored.

## Status, 30 Sep 2026

The last commit packaged the static build for hosting, and there's one uncommitted change in `test.mjs`. It hasn't been tested on a real iPad or with Italian device voices.
